    What do these words mean?
    Alert level (Green / Orange / Red)
    Set by GDACS, a UN and European Commission system. It estimates how many people are exposed and how able the country is to cope. Red means international help will probably be needed.
    Magnitude
    How much energy an earthquake released. Each step up (6 to 7) is about 32 times more energy. Damage also depends on depth and how close towns are.
    Hurricane, typhoon, cyclone
    The same kind of storm with different names by ocean. Categories 1–5 rate the wind; Category 3 and above are "major".
    Storm surge
    Sea water pushed onto land by a storm's winds. It causes most hurricane deaths.
    Forecast wind zones
    The shaded areas around a cyclone show where winds of at least 60, 90 or 120 km/h are expected along its forecast path.
