    man disaster-words
    Alert level
    Set by GDACS (UN + European Commission) from how many people are exposed and how well the country can cope. Red = international help likely needed.
    Magnitude
    Energy released by an earthquake. Each step up (6 → 7) is about 32× more energy. Shallow quakes shake the surface harder.
    Hurricane / typhoon / cyclone
    Same storm, different ocean. Categories 1–5 rate the wind; 3+ is "major".
    Storm surge
    Sea water pushed onto land by the wind. The deadliest part of most hurricanes.
    Wind zones
    The shaded bands around a cyclone's path show where winds of 60, 90 and 120 km/h are forecast.
    Air quality (US AQI)
    0–50 good · 51–100 moderate · 101–150 unhealthy for sensitive groups · 151+ unhealthy for everyone.
